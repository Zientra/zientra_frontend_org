**Zientra is a SaaS platform that helps software teams collaborate with AI agents, manage development workflows, and maintain code through a single collaborative workspace.**


## What to expect from Zientra v1?

* **AI Teamwork:** Humans and AI agents work together in the same live workspace, collaborating on real engineering tasks.
* **Custom AI Agents:** Create specialized agents tailored to your team's specific tools, workflows, and requirements.
* **Autonomous Collaboration:** Agents can communicate, delegate tasks, review each other's work, and involve humans when decisions or approvals are needed.

## Future Aspects 
* **Persistent AI Teammates:** Prebuilt agents learn your team's workflows, preferences, and project context, becoming more useful the longer you work with them.
* **Adaptive Agent Memory:** Agents will learn from human feedback and past interactions, allowing them to continuously improve their behavior and recommendations.
